# My Breakfast

## Basic Info
A tool to help you decide what to eat for breakfast, taking into account what you hate, what you actually have, and how much time you have to prep.

I built this with Next.js.

(Note: The number of listed meals is very limited, and the ingredient lists of some may be simplified. This is more of a fun project than an actual practical real-life tool.)

## How to Run
To get a customized list of meals based on what you "hate," what you actually have, and how much time you have to prep:
1. Click the "Let's Get Started!" button.
2. Click/select all the ingredients you don't enjoy eating (aka "hate").
3. Click "Next"
4. Click/select all the ingredients that you have available.
5. Click "Next"
6. Type into the input field how many minutes you have available to prepare your food.
7. Click "Next"
8. View your results! (There's a chance that it won't give you any meal suggestions because none of my listed meals your constraints.)
